# How can I contact support?

Source: https://docs.worm.wtf/faq/support-community/contact-support



You can reach us on **Discord** - join our server at **[https://discord.gg/jQgQssQHT9](https://discord.gg/jQgQssQHT9)** and open a ticket in the support channel.

Alternatively, you can open a ticket via **[https://t.me/wormsupportbot](https://t.me/wormsupportbot)**.

We respond quickly and try not to ruin your day.

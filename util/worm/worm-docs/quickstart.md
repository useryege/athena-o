# Quickstart

> Connect your wallet and make your first prediction on Worm.

## Before you start

Worm runs on Solana and settles in **USDC**. You'll need a Solana wallet ([Phantom](https://phantom.app), [Solflare](https://solflare.com), or [Backpack](https://backpack.app)) with USDC on Solana loaded in it. If you don't have USDC on Solana yet, you can bridge or buy it through your wallet app before continuing.

## Connect your wallet

Go to [worm.wtf](https://worm.wtf) and connect your wallet using the button in the top right. Once connected, your USDC balance will appear there.

## Find a market

The homepage is a live market feed. Use the filter tabs to narrow things down (Trending, New, Leverage, or Ending soon) and the category tabs to browse by topic: Politics, Sports, Crypto, Tech, Finance, or WTF. The search bar at the top works too.

## Place a trade

Open a market and choose the side you want to trade on. On user-created markets you can place a limit order at a specific price, or fill immediately at the best available price.

## Create your own market

Click the **+** button in the top right, or type your idea directly into the prompt on the creation screen. Worm's AI will draft the market rules, set a resolution deadline, and find an image automatically.

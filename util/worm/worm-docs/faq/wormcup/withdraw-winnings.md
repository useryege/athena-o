# When can I withdraw my WormCup winnings?

Any cash prizes you win are credited to your **WormCup profile balance**, which shows what you've earned.

**Withdrawals open after the tournament.** There's no cash-out button in the app during the event, so your balance is the running record of your winnings until then.

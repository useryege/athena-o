# What are WormCup points worth?

Your **tournament points** rank you on the leaderboard. Top players on the all-time leaderboard share an **end-of-tournament prize pool** paid in real money.

The exact prize amounts and payout details are announced closer to the end of the tournament. In the meantime, every prediction you make and every trade you place builds your standing.

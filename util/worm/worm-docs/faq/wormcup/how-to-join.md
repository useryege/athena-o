# How do I join WormCup?

Open [t.me/wormcupbot](https://t.me/wormcupbot) in Telegram and tap **Play WormCup** to launch the Mini App.

There's no wallet or sign-up needed to start. Your Telegram account is your identity. See [What is WormCup?](/wormcup/what-is-wormcup) for a full overview.

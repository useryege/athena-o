# What's the difference between tournament and activity points?

**Tournament points** are your rank score. You earn them from correct match predictions and from trading on [worm.wtf](https://worm.wtf). They're the only points that rank you on the leaderboard.

**Activity points** are fuel. You earn them from tapping, tasks, and inviting friends, and you spend them to power up your predictions (1,000 per level). They don't affect your rank and they never expire.

See [Points & Leaderboards](/wormcup/points-and-leaderboards) for the full breakdown.

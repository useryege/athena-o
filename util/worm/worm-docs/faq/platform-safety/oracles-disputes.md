# Does Worm use oracles or have a dispute mechanism?

Not yet. Currently all markets are resolved by Worm's AI, with a human review step before any resolution is finalized. There is no third-party oracle system (like UMA) and no open dispute process.

This is a known limitation. As Worm scales, introducing decentralized oracle and dispute mechanisms is a priority. It's a necessary step for the platform to be fully permissionless at scale. It's on the roadmap.

For now, the human review step is the safeguard against incorrect resolutions. If you believe a market was resolved incorrectly, [contact support](https://discord.gg/jQgQssQHT9) and the team will investigate.

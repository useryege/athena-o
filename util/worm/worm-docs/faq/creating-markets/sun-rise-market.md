# Can I open a market on 'Will the sun rise tomorrow?'

Yes. As long as a market follows platform rules, you can open it.

Markets like this are effectively useless: the uncertainty of the market is very low, so liquidity only ever appears on one side. Everyone buys "Yes," nobody buys "No," and the price never moves.

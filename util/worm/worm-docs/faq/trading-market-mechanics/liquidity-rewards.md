# How do I earn liquidity rewards?

On a hand-picked set of markets, Worm pays out a **daily reward pool** to traders who place limit orders that keep the market liquid. You earn a share just for keeping a qualifying order open, with no need to win the bet.

To qualify, an order must:

* Be a **limit order** resting on the book (market orders don't count).
* Sit within the market's **Max Spread** of the current midpoint price.
* Meet the market's **Min Shares** minimum size (unfilled shares only).

Spot eligible markets by the **blue diamond** on the market card, or browse them all under the **Rewards** tab.

Rewards accrue **by the second**, for every second your order qualifies, and are paid out **once a day at the end of the day (UTC)**, straight to your wallet. If a day's earnings come to less than **\$1**, they're discarded rather than paid, and they don't roll over. Your earnings show up on the **Liquidity Rewards** card and **Rewards** tab on your profile.

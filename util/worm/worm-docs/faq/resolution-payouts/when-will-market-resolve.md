# When will my market resolve?

Markets resolve **at or after their deadline**, never before, even if the outcome is already obvious.

How long after the deadline depends on the market. Sports match markets resolve within a few minutes of the event ending, using live data feeds. All other markets go through an AI resolution step followed by a human review. Simpler markets resolve faster, more complex ones may take longer.

If the outcome isn't determinable at the first attempt, the market is retried automatically after a waiting period.

For a full breakdown, see [Settling & Payouts](/trading/settling-and-payouts).

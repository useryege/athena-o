# Is there a referral program?

Yes, in **WormCup**, Worm's World Cup Telegram Mini App. Inviting friends earns you activity points and enters you into a weekly leaderboard for USDC cash prizes. See [Referrals & Prizes](/wormcup/referrals-and-prizes).

A platform-wide referral program for worm.wtf itself is still planned and will be introduced in a future update.

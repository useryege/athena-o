# Points & Leaderboards

> Tournament points vs activity points, weekly and all-time leaderboards, and prizes.

WormCup has **two kinds of points**, and they do different jobs. Keeping them straight is the key to the game.

## Tournament points — your rank

**Tournament points** are your score. They're the only thing that ranks you on the leaderboard, and they come from **skill**:

* Correct match predictions (200–2,000 per match, multiplied by any levels you apply)
* Trading on [worm.wtf](https://worm.wtf) (5,000 standard / 10,000 leveraged)

Think of tournament points as your rank score. Grinding taps or invites won't move them.

## Activity points — your fuel

**Activity points** are what you spend to [power up your predictions](/wormcup/making-predictions#powering-up-your-picks) (1,000 per level). They don't rank you, and they **never expire**.

### Earning activity points

* **Tapping** — tap the football in the app. Every **1,000 taps** earns **1,000 activity points**. Your daily tap limit grows as you keep a daily check-in streak.
* **Tasks** — complete quests like following Worm on X or joining the Telegram channel and Discord.
* **Invites** — each friend who joins earns you activity points. See [Referrals & Prizes](/wormcup/referrals-and-prizes).

<Note>
  Tapping earns activity points, not cash and not rank. It's there to fuel your predictions.
</Note>

## Leaderboards

The tournament points leaderboard has two views:

* **Weekly** — resets each week, so newcomers always have a fresh shot at the top.
* **All-time** — never resets, and tracks your total across the whole tournament.

A weekly reset never takes away points you've already earned all-time.

## Prizes

Top players on the **all-time** leaderboard share an **end-of-tournament prize pool**.

<Note>
  The prize pool is real money. The exact amounts and payout details are announced closer to the end of the tournament.
</Note>

Any prizes you win are credited to your **WormCup profile balance**, which shows what you've earned. **Withdrawals open after the tournament.**

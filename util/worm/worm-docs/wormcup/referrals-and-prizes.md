# Referrals & Prizes

> Invite friends to earn activity points and compete for weekly USDC cash prizes.

Inviting friends is the fastest way to earn in WormCup. Every friend who joins earns you **activity points**, and the more people you bring in, the higher you climb on the **weekly referral cash leaderboard**.

## Your referral link

Get your invite link from the app or by sending **/invite** to [t.me/wormcupbot](https://t.me/wormcupbot). Share it anywhere. When someone joins through it and connects Telegram, they count as your referral.

Each successful invite earns you activity points, which you can spend to [power up your predictions](/wormcup/making-predictions#powering-up-your-picks).

## Weekly cash leaderboard

Referrals also enter you into a weekly competition for **USDC cash prizes**, ranked by how many friends you bring in that week.

* The competition runs in **weekly rounds on New York time**, with each round closing at **11:59 PM NY**.
* **Top referrers** win cash prizes by rank.
* A **jackpot** is drawn at random among players with **3 or more referrals** that week.

<Note>
  The exact cutoff day and the prize pool change from week to week and are announced each round in the Telegram channel. Only real referrals count, farmed or fake accounts are filtered out.
</Note>

## Getting paid

Cash prizes are credited to your **WormCup profile balance** as a record of what you've won. **Withdrawals open after the tournament** — there's no cash-out button in the app during the event.

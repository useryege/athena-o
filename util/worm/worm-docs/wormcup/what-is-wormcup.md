# What is WormCup?

> Worm's Telegram Mini App for the 2026 World Cup: predict matches, earn points, win prizes.

WormCup is Worm's Telegram Mini App for the **2026 FIFA World Cup**. Predict match results, earn points for every prediction, climb the leaderboards, and invite friends for weekly cash prizes, all from inside Telegram.

Every prediction earns points, win or lose. Being right earns more, but showing up always pays.

## Getting started

Open [**t.me/wormcupbot**](https://t.me/wormcupbot) in Telegram and tap **Play WormCup** to launch the app. From the chat you can also use:

* **/play** — open the app
* **/invite** — get your referral link

No wallet or sign-up is required to start playing. Your Telegram account is your identity.

## The core loop

1. **Predict** the World Cup matches. Every prediction earns **tournament points** based on how accurate it is.
2. **Power up** your picks by spending **activity points** you earn from tapping, tasks, and invites.
3. **Climb** the weekly and all-time leaderboards. Top all-time players share an end-of-tournament prize pool.
4. **Invite** friends to earn more and compete on the weekly referral cash leaderboard.
5. **Trade on [worm.wtf](https://worm.wtf)** to earn a large bonus of tournament points.

<Note>
  WormCup is a points-first game. Your rank comes from correct predictions and trading, not from grinding taps or invites. Prizes are credited to your in-app balance; withdrawals open after the tournament.
</Note>

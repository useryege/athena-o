# Squads

> Team up with friends, join squads, and pool your points on matches.

Squads let you play WormCup with friends. Create your own or join an existing one, then compete together.

## Creating or joining a squad

* **Create a squad** and you become its owner. You'll get an invite link to share.
* **Join a squad** by opening someone's invite link (`t.me/wormcupbot?startapp=sq_...`).

A squad holds up to **20 members**, and you can be part of more than one squad at a time. As the owner, you can remove members, but you can't leave a squad you own.

## Squad competitions

Squad competitions let members put their **tournament points** on the line together on a single match. Everyone who stakes contributes to a shared pot, and when the match resolves the pot is paid out based on how accurate each member's prediction was.

<Note>
  Only tournament points earned from predictions and trading can be staked in a squad competition, not the activity points you use to power up picks.
</Note>

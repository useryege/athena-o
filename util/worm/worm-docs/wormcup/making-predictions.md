# Making Predictions

> How to predict World Cup matches, how points are scored, and how to power up your picks.

Predicting matches is the heart of WormCup. Every prediction earns **tournament points**, the score that ranks you on the leaderboard. The more accurate your call, the more you earn.

## Making a pick

Open a match and enter your prediction:

* **The exact score** (for example, 2–1), or the **winner or draw** on matches that only ask for an outcome.
* **The first goalscorer**, by jersey number. This is required unless you're predicting a **0–0** draw.

You can make **one prediction per match**, and a match **locks at kickoff**. After that, predictions can no longer be placed or changed.

## How points are scored

After the match, your prediction earns tournament points based on how close it was:

| Result                                   | Points |
| ---------------------------------------- | -----: |
| Exact score **and** correct first scorer |  2,000 |
| Exact score                              |  1,000 |
| Correct goal difference                  |    700 |
| Correct winner or draw                   |    500 |
| Participated (wrong prediction)          |    200 |

<Note>
  Scoring uses the **regulation (90-minute) result** only. Extra time and penalties don't count toward your prediction. If a match goes beyond regulation, the score at the end of 90 minutes is what's graded.
</Note>

## Powering up your picks

You can multiply the tournament points a prediction earns by adding **levels** before kickoff.

* Each level costs **1,000 activity points**.
* You can add up to **10 levels** on a single prediction.
* Levels are applied **once per pick**, before the match locks.

The multiplier applies to whatever your prediction earns, even a wrong pick at the 200-point participation tier. For example, an exact-score prediction (1,000 base) with 3 levels applied earns **6,000** tournament points.

Activity points are the "fuel" you spend here. You earn them from tapping, tasks, and invites. See [Points & Leaderboards](/wormcup/points-and-leaderboards) for how to stock up.

## Earn points by trading

Trading on [worm.wtf](https://worm.wtf) also earns tournament points, with no action needed inside the Mini App:

* **5,000** points for a trade on a standard market
* **10,000** points for a leveraged trade

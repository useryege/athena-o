# Notifications

Source: https://docs.worm.wtf/platform/notifications

Stay on top of your positions and markets with Telegram alerts.

Worm's notification system lets you receive real-time alerts on Telegram, so you don't have to watch the screen to stay informed.

<video />

## Setting up Telegram notifications

Connect your Telegram account from your profile settings on Worm. Once connected, you'll start receiving alerts for your activity automatically.

## What you'll be notified about

### Position alerts

Receive notifications when key events happen on your open positions, including fills, resolution, and payouts.

### Price alerts

Set a price target on any market and get notified when it's reached. You can configure alerts for:

* **Price rises above** a specific level (e.g. notify me when Yes crosses 70¢)
* **Price falls below** a specific level (e.g. notify me when Yes drops below 40¢)

This is particularly useful for leveraged positions. Set an alert near your liquidation price to give yourself time to add margin before the position is force-closed.

# What is Worm?

Source: https://docs.worm.wtf/getting-started/what-is-worm

A permissionless prediction layer on Solana, powered by AI.

Worm is a permissionless prediction market platform built on Solana. Trade on events you have conviction about with leverage, or create your own markets and earn from every trade on them, all AI-powered and fully on-chain.

## Leveraged prediction markets

The core problem with prediction markets is upside compression. When a market is already at 80%, the maximum return on a correct prediction is 25%, even if you're highly confident. Worm's leveraged markets solve this.

Take 1x–3x positions on curated markets with deep liquidity, sourced by aggregating order books from established prediction market platforms. Worm supplies the additional capital for your position, and you earn a multiplied return if you're right.

These markets are actively maintained by Worm, with tight spreads and fast resolution.

## Permissionless market creation

Anyone can create a prediction market on Worm for free. No approval process, no listing fee. You describe the event you want to predict, and Worm's AI helps you build a well-formed market with clear resolution rules, automatically.

Markets you create earn you half of every trading fee collected on them, paid out immediately as trades happen.

## What makes Worm different

**Leverage on prediction markets.** No other platform lets you take amplified positions on prediction markets. Worm is the first.

**Free and permissionless.** Other platforms charge significant fees to create markets. Worm charges nothing.

**AI-powered.** Creating and resolving markets is genuinely hard to do well. Worm's AI handles the heavy lifting: drafting rules, finding resolution sources, and settling outcomes, so you can focus on the prediction.

**Built on Solana.** Everything is on-chain. Your funds are locked in smart contracts until markets resolve. No custodians, no middlemen.

**Order-book model.** Prices are set by real bids and asks, not an algorithm. For leveraged markets, Worm aggregates its own order book with those of major prediction market platforms to find the best available price.

## Built to scale

Today, Worm handles leveraged trading, market creation, and AI-powered resolution. As the platform grows, more order books will be aggregated, oracle and dispute mechanisms will be introduced, and liquidity pools will let users earn yield as market makers.

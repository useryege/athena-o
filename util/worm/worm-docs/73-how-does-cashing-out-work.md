# How does cashing out work?

Source: https://docs.worm.wtf/faq/trading-market-mechanics/cash-out



Cashing out on Worm means **selling your position back into the market**: it's a trade, not a withdrawal.

When you sell, your shares are matched against buyers in the order book and your PnL is realized at the sale price. After the trade executes, funds typically appear in your wallet within about a minute as the on-chain transaction processes.

If you've waited more than a minute and still don't see the funds, [open a support ticket](https://discord.gg/jQgQssQHT9) and the team will investigate.
